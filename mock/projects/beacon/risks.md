# Beacon Risk Register

## Housing Corrosion

Salt air corrosion of the enclosure seal is the leading cause of past field failures; two units already needed early housing replacement.

## Firmware Regression

A watchdog timer bug could mask a genuine main-loop stall.

## Mitigation

Marine-grade fasteners and a yearly seal inspection.
